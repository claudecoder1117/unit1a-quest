|---|---|
| `js/gen/contract.js` (≈ 580 lines) | The shared contract. `makeGen(template, draw, options)` → `gen(seed, params)`: deterministic re-roll **from the same stream**, capped at `MAX_ATTEMPTS = 200` draws, then the template's **fixed exemplar** (`fallback:true`); ids `T-<template>#<6 hex of the uint32 stream seed>`; `templateVersion` on every item; structural validation (`validateItem`) + the template's own solver check (`verify`) before an item is returned. | `js/gen/csarith.js` | `T-csarith` — chains of depth 1–3 ("Find the supplement of the complement of 21.5°"). One `num` part with the S3 `asks` chain and its `distractors`. **A complement is only ever taken of a value < 90 AND never straight after a supplement**, so comp-of-supp cannot be generated. Depth 3 is the "the complement of the complement is the angle you started with" item (the only legal 3-chains are comp-comp-supp and comp-supp-supp — see §4.1); its intermediates are written out as explicit `misconceptions[]` because two `comp` stages cannot share one distractor name. |
| `js/gen/cslin.js` (≈ 710 lines) | `T-cs-lin` — the teacher's sentence frames with the numbers swapped: S2's nine (wp-01 · wp-02 · wp-03 · wp-04 · wp-13 · wp-15 · wp-09 · wp-06/ang-02/ang-03 · ang-11) **plus F10 = wp-08's "half of the difference"**, which none of the nine reproduces. Every item = `equation` slot + a `num`/`multi` answer. Asks: S2's `{angle, comp, supp, larger, supp-of-comp}` plus the teacher's own `{angle+comp, angle+supp, pair, smaller, supp-of-smaller, comp-of-smaller, supp-of-larger}`; a frame narrows its asks per draw so a supplementary pair is never asked for "the supplement of the smaller" (that is just the other angle). Alternates ("let x be the complement / the supplement / the other angle") are the same frame function applied to `(90−x)` / `(180−x)` and are re-solved with poly.js before they ship. Equation traps: the comp↔supp swap (built by re-running the frame with the two helpers exchanged) and, on F5, the grouping misread `3(x−5)` → `3x−5`. |
| `js/gen/csratio.js` | `T-cs-ratio` — S2's three shapes: **parts** (`p+q ∈ {3,4,5,6,9,10,12,15,18,20,30,36}` dividing 180 or 90 — ang-07 · wp-05), **supp:comp** (`x = (180q − 90p)/(q − p)` whole and in (0, 90), which forces `p > 2q` — ang-08 · wp-11) and **angle:supp asking angle:comp reduced** (wp-10, a `ratio` part). |
| `js/gen/csquad.js` | `T-cs-quad` — the S3 `rootcase` chain (`equation → roots → reject → num`) with the **both-valid / one-invalid coin flip**: `x(90−x) = P` (both roots are angles, reason "both valid" — wp-12) vs the uncancelled `q·x(180−x) = p·x(90−x)` (x = 0 falls out, reason "zero angle" — ang-09). The shape is chosen once and the parameters re-drawn inside it, so the flip stays 50/50 despite different rejection rates. |
| `data/templates.js` | The registry for **all 21 templates** (T07a's 4, T07b's 7, T07c's 10) + the shared API — see §2 and §5. |

## 4. Decisions worth knowing

1. **Depth-3 csarith chains always contain a repeat, and that is the item.** With `comp∘comp = supp∘supp = id`
   and "no comp after a supp", the only legal 3-chains are `comp,comp,supp` and `comp,supp,supp`; every longer
   word collapses. Rather than drop depth 3 (S2 asks for 1–3) the solution names the collapse out loud
   ("the complement of the complement is the angle you started with" — asn-23's insight). Depth 3 is ~18 % of draws.
2. **`asks` is the chain, and the chain must be unambiguous.** `num.js` maps distractor NAMES to chain stages, so
   a chain with two `comp` stages cannot be diagnosed from the map alone. Depth-3 items therefore ship explicit
   `misconceptions[]` for every intermediate (they are matched before the chain machinery), and depth 1–2 lean on
   S3's own wording ("That's the complement — the question asks for the supplement of the complement (180 − 68.5)").
3. **An empty `misconceptions: []` on a PART shadows the item's list** (`num.js: part.misconceptions ?? ctx…` —
   `[]` is not nullish). Parts here only carry the key when they actually have entries. This bit me on the
   `roots` part of `T-cs-quad`; worth knowing for T07b/T07c and any content ticket.
4. **Alternates are generated, then verified.** Each is the frame rebuilt with the angle written as `(90−x)` or
   `(180−x)`, its root re-solved with poly.js, and kept only if the solver agrees — so "let x be the complement"
   is accepted with its own `mustMention` and never by accident.
5. **H3 is never the answer.** `linearForm()` reads the collected step (`8x = 228`) off the emitted equation, but
   when the equation collects to `1·x = …` (which IS the answer) H3 falls back to the generic wording.
6. **No degenerate asks.** A frame narrows its ask list per draw: the supplement of one of two supplementary
   angles is the other angle, so that question is never asked (same for the complement of a complementary pair).
7. **The coin flip is protected.** `T-cs-quad` picks the shape first and re-draws parameters *inside* it, because
   the two shapes reject at different rates and re-flipping per attempt biased it 78/22.
8. **Products stay factorable by hand**: the smaller root of the product shape is drawn from 2–25 (biased small),
   so `x² − 90x + P` has a findable factor pair, like the teacher's 344 = 4 × 86.
9. **Every generated measure is an integer or a half, strictly inside (0, 180)**, and `x ∈ (0, 90)` wherever a
   complement is taken. Pair frames solve for the SMALLER angle so `x` stays acute even for supplementary pairs.

## 5. Integration: the `data/templates.js` collision (read this first)

T07b and T07c ran **concurrently** with this ticket. `site/data/templates.js` and `tests/gen.test.mjs` had already
been created by T07b when T07a wrote its own `templates.js` — **T07b's registrations were overwritten**. They were
reconstructed here from each generator's own item metadata (`module/sheet/forCard/tier/par/skills/needs`), and the
converged file now registers all 21 templates and exports the API both other lanes' tests use. What changed, so the
integrator and the other owners can audit it:

* **T07b's 7 entries were rebuilt** (`T-fig-xlines-L/Q`, `T-fig-system`, `T-fig-bisect-L/Q`, `T-seg-mid`,
  `T-fig-pairs`) with `forCard` + `forCards`. T07b has since reworked those generators onto `makeGen`, so the
  entries now register the generator **directly** (`gen: genXlinesL, version: genXlinesL.version`) — the
  `fromRngGen` adapter that briefly bridged their older `gen(rng)` shape is gone, and all 21 templates now share
  one calling convention. **Their generator files were not touched**, and their whole test block passes unchanged.
  *Request → T07b:* re-read the labels/blurbs/`partTypes` on your seven entries — those are the only fields I had
  to invent (everything else came from your items).
* **T07b made two one-line changes inside `js/gen/contract.js`**, both marked `T07b:` and both kept: the id
  regex is case-insensitive (so `T-fig-xlines-L#…` validates) and an `equation` part may carry a two-variable
  `system` instead of a single `canonical` (doc-07's form). Neither affects T07a's templates.
* **T07c's block is preserved verbatim**, minus two lines: its duplicate `import {mulberry32, rngFrom, seedTag}`
  (the file-level import now serves the whole module — a second `seedTag` binding is a SyntaxError) and its
  `templateIds.push(...)` bookkeeping (`templateIds()` is a function now; the object is the registry). Its
  temporary "compat shim" is gone: `getTemplate`, `templatesFor` and `modelOf` are real exports of the API.
* **Two assertions in T07c's test block were updated** (not deleted) for the converged API: `templateIds()` is
  called, and `generate('T-does-not-exist')` now *throws* instead of returning null. Its "frozen seed replays"
  case now checks `seedKey === '<template>|<seed>'`.
* `templatesFor(cardId)` returns **entries** (T07b's shape); `templatesForCard/Skill/Module` return **ids**
  (T07c's shape). Both are exported deliberately and documented in the file header.

## 6. Open issues

1. **`wp-14` and `wp-16` have no template.** Their sentences are ratio *expressions* ("one less than half of the
   complement … to the angle itself is 1:2"), which none of S2's three ratio shapes covers. They are the only two
   M4/M5 originals without an Infinite view; a 4th csratio shape would close it.
2. **`T-csarith` has no `equation` slot** — it is arithmetic (M3/CSARITH), so Boss B1 and BLITZ get a bare `num`.
   That matches S2 ("none fixed — `T-csarith` chains depth 1–3 (BLITZ + cards)").
3. The equation slot is emitted as `optional: true` (skippable on a Card, S3). **Boss B4 must enforce it itself**
   (T12): nothing in the data marks it required.
4. `tests/gen.test.mjs` is ≈ 43 s at 5 000 seeds × 21 templates. The T07a block runs its two expensive checks
   (re-solving every alternate, grading every misconception) on every 4th item — ≈ 1 250 per template — while the
   cheap invariants run on all 5 000. If T17 wants a faster suite, `GEN_SEEDS` is the knob.
5. `meta.params` on `T-cs-lin` records the frame's own constants (m, k, j…); nothing reads them yet. They exist so
   a future "more like this one" button can re-draw the same frame (`generate(id, seed, {frame})` already works).
