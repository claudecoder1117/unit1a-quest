## 4. Decisions worth knowing
1. **Block order is the spec's; the same-skill spread swaps forward across blocks.** `spreadSkills` walks the page
   and, at the third consecutive item of one skill, pulls the nearest later item of a different skill forward
   (checking it does not create a run behind it). Only when *no* other skill exists on the page is the surplus
   dropped to `carried` — dues stay due, new cards stay new, so nothing is lost; the invariant is absolute.
2. **Session cap (`LIMITS.pageMax = 20`).** The S1 structure allows 2 + 3 + 12 + 12 + 3 = 32 items; on a
   review-heavy day that is a 45-minute page against a 10–25-minute session. Reviews are never cut (they are
   the spec's priority); the new-card count shrinks to `pageMax − reviews`, never below `qMin = 4`, and the rest
   lands on the day's second Page (`pageIndex` 1, a different seed). T14's plan can pass an explicit `q` and the
   cap steps aside (`opts.q` is honoured verbatim).
3. **"≤ 3 modules" = ≤ 3 modules of *main* work (tier ≥ 2): up to 2 geometry + 1 algebra in topo order**, so a
   page always alternates families when both exist (S1) and M10 is on the page from day 1 (S7 says "by day 2").
   Micro-cards (tier 1 recall: voc/def/fact/cls, ASN) are flash items — they are drawn from the chosen modules
   first and then from M1/M9, and interleave 1-in-4. Counting them as a fourth module would have starved M4 word
   problems behind 54 ASN cards for two weeks.
4. **Topo order, not packet order, and `needs` on the pool.** `newCardPool` sorts by `skills.js topoOrder()` rank
   of the card's primary skill, then sheet order inside a skill; a card whose `needs` are unmet is simply not in the
   pool (it stays openable in the Binder — Global rule 1).
5. **`rename` on reviews is applied to `pairs` cards only** (ang-wu-1..5 — the answer *is* the letters). For
   ang-10/doc-07/… the stem, the `cases` columns and the wedge ids all carry letters, so a rename needs T09's stem
   and answer-key renaming; `renameFor(card, rng)` is exported and ready for that. See Requests → T09.
6. **Boss readiness is generic**: every module of the boss has all originals cleared *and* every family tile
   ≥ Bronze. For B3 that means M10's 18 + M11's `quad-01..03` + the four tiles (the spec's parenthesis says M11/M12
   "have no originals", but `quad-*` are M11 originals in the data; they are tier-2 and quick).
7. **Mock offer (S7 "from D−4")** is the primary button only after today's goal is met, when no Mock/Baseline has
   ever scored `A`, and D ≤ 4; the Mock stays one tap away in the secondary row every day.
8. **Post-test** = D < 0 or `now > testTime + 90 min` on test day (S7); the primary becomes "After the test ·
   Binder" and `housekeep` drops every frozen Variant. The "How did it go?" entry is T14's.
9. **Rematch for untemplated cards** (asn/voc/def/fact) is not a Variant — the Card itself is bucket 0 and due, so
   the review block carries it; `pendingRematches` only yields templated entries. T09 clears the error entry with
   `clearRematch(save, id)` on the next first-try clear.
10. **`accuracyOf(run)`** reads `accuracy` or `score/scoreMax` so a 10-item Baseline scored `/10` or `/100` both
    work; the run must be `status:'done'`.
11. **Home writes nothing unless housekeeping changed something** (decay, prune, goal, today's forecast point). The
    dry pass runs on a `structuredClone` of the save; only a real change goes through `update()`.
