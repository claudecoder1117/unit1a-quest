**The `equation` part (parts[0] on every card, id `setup`).** `{canonical, var:'x', mustMention, roots, text, alternates}`:
- `canonical` is LHS − RHS in the site's expression grammar, written *as the sentence says it* (`180-x-(10+9x)`, `x/(180-x)-5/7`,
  `((90-x)/2-1)/x-1/2`, `x(90-x)-344`), never pre-simplified, so the `mustMention` guard (S3) and the display `text` agree.
  Every canonical is the teacher's own setup from the key pages (wp-05 is `x/(180 − x) = 5/7` with x the smaller angle, not a
  parts equation — that is what the teacher wrote).
- `roots` — the intended root(s) as strings (`['21.5']`, `['86','4']`), so the S3 fallback ("LHS − RHS = 0 at every intended root,
  ≠ 0 at r+1, r+2") needs no solving at runtime.
- `alternates[]` — other correct setups where x names a different quantity (`{canonical, mustMention, roots, means, text}`): the
  complement / supplement / larger angle / one ratio part. wp-04's `90 − x = 6 + 2x`, wp-05's `5x + 7x = 180`, wp-10's
  `3x + 7x = 180`, wp-11's `7x − 2x = 90` are all legitimate and a student who writes one must not be marked wrong. **The
  equation grader should accept the canonical OR any alternate** (each alternate carries its own `mustMention`).
- `text` — the setup in the teacher's notation (`½(180 − x − x) = 3.5`), for the "skip setup" reveal / solution screen only.

**`num` parts.** `asks` is the S3 chain from the solved angle (`['comp','supp']` = supplement of the complement; `['smaller','supp']`;
`['larger']`; `['comp']`), `distractors` names every intermediate a student can stop at, computed by the solver and asserted
against it: vocabulary `angle` (the sentence's x), `comp`, `supp`, `smaller`, `larger` — exactly the names T03's `num.js`
`chainOf`/`diagnoseDistractors` understand. No distractor equals the answer (asserted). `multi` fields on wp-01/02/03 carry
their own `asks:[key]` + `distractors` (T03 `multi.js` field shape), so a supplement typed into the angle box gets
"That's the supplement — the question asks for the angle" and a swap gets the grader's own swapped-fields line.

**Hints** H1 = the relationship and the phrase-to-expression mapping, H2 = the setup (S3: "H2 setup"; the equation slot is
skippable on Cards), H3 = one step from the end — never a graded value (asserted: no hint contains any final answer or, on
wp-12, either root). **Solutions** mirror the teacher's key line by line (`180 − x − 14 = 3(90 − x)` → `180 − x − 14 = 270 − 3x` →
`2x − 14 = 90` → `2x = 104` → `x = 52` → `supp = 128°`), ending on the teacher's answer; wp-12 factors as the key does
(`0 = (x − 86)(x − 4)`), states that both roots are valid and why, then `supp of 4 = 176°`.

**Misconceptions** `{part, field?, answer, tag | requestedTag, msg, gives?}` — 92 entries (81 tagged), every `tag` catalogued (T06g scan is
clean for this file): `gave-angle/-complement/-supplement/-smaller/-larger`, `stopped-early` (chain stops, incl. S3's wp-07 line
"That's the complement — the question asks for the supplement of the complement (180 − 68.5)"), `used-90-for-supp`,
`used-180-for-comp`, `wrong-side-supp`, `reversed-ratio`, `ratio-as-measure`. Equation-part entries hold the wrong equation as
text plus `gives` (the x it yields — verified by solving it; `null` when degenerate) and are asserted **not** equivalent to the
canonical or any alternate (a reversed wp-10 ratio turned out to be the "x = supplement" alternate and was dropped).
11 entries are grouping misreads with no catalogue tag (`3x − 5` for `3(x − 5)`, `180 − (x − 14)`, `x(180 − x) = 344` as a sum…):
they carry `requestedTag:'grouping'` and no `tag` — they still match by value and show their `msg`; see Requests.
No entry on a `roots` part (the roots grader's subset path is a free `almost` and never reveals the other root — S3), no entry
that duplicates the grader's own field-swap or sign diagnosis, and no `msg` states its own part's answer (asserted).

## Decisions worth knowing
- **Canonicals are the teacher's setups, unsimplified**, and `alternates` exist because "let x be the other angle" is a correct
  setup the S3 polynomial comparison would otherwise mark wrong (wp-04: `90 − x = 6 + 2x`; wp-09: x = the larger angle; ratio
  problems: the parts form).
- **wp-12 has no `reject` stage**: both roots give positive angles and are the same pair (4 · 86 = 344); S2 says "rootcase roots →
  num". The `roots` part carries a `note` saying so; the num part is chained with `of:'x'`.
- **`gives:null`** marks a wrong equation that is degenerate (`(180 − x) − (180 − x) = 24`) or non-linear/non-factorable; the grader's
  "That gives x = …" line only applies to degree-1 equations anyway (S3).
- **Skills are single-valued** (`['CS-LIN']` / `['CS-RATIO']` / `['CS-QUAD']`) so the composer's "never 3 consecutive items of one
  skill" and mastery attribution are unambiguous; M4's Ratio room is CS-RATIO even where the ratio reduces to a linear equation
  (wp-14, wp-16), as S2 assigns.
- `teacherNo` is not stored (`sheets.numbering('wp-07')` → `"7."`); `src` is `'§3 #7'` like T06e's `'§6 #1'`.
